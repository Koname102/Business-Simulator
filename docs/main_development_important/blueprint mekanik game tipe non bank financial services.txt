📊 BLUEPRINT: FINTECH (P2P Lending)
Konsep Bisnis
Player adalah platform P2P Lending yang menghubungkan peminjam (borrower) dengan uang perusahaan. Player approve/reject loan applications.
Cara Kerja (Real Life)
Borrower → Apply Loan (misal 10 juta, 18% bunga, 12 bulan)
         ↓
      Review → Check credit score
         ↓
   Approve? → YES: Disburse uang
         ↓
  Borrower → Cicilan bulanan (pokok + bunga)
         ↓
    Player → Dapat profit dari bunga
Gameplay Loop
1. LOAN APPLICATION MASUK
   - Setiap X detik, ada borrower baru apply
   - Tampil info: Nama, jumlah pinjaman, durasi, credit score
   
2. PLAYER DECISION
   - APPROVE → Uang keluar (disbursed)
   - REJECT → Tidak ada perubahan
   
3. REPAYMENT PHASE (Jika approved)
   - Setiap bulan (in-game), borrower bayar cicilan
   - Ada kemungkinan DEFAULT (gagal bayar)
   
4. OUTCOME
   - PAID FULL → Player profit (bunga)
   - DEFAULTED → Player loss (pokok hilang)
Income Sources (Cara Dapat Uang)
SourceAmountTimingNotesLoan RepaymentPokok + BungaPer bulanMain incomeInterest Income12-24% per tahunDari repaymentPure profit
Formula Income:
Total Repayment = Principal × (1 + Rate)^Years

Contoh:
Loan: Rp 10 juta
Rate: 18% per tahun
Duration: 12 bulan (1 tahun)

Total Repayment = 10,000,000 × (1 + 0.18)^1 = Rp 11,800,000
Profit = 11,800,000 - 10,000,000 = Rp 1,800,000 (18%)
Expense Sources (Cara Keluar Uang)
ExpenseAmountTimingNotesLoan DisbursementPrincipalSaat approveUang keluarDefault LossSisa unpaidSaat defaultTotal lossOperational Cost1-5% revenuePer bulanMarketing, staff, server
Formula Expense:
Disbursement = Principal (saat approve loan)
Default Loss = Principal - Paid Amount
Operational = Total Revenue × 3%
Risk Management
Credit Score System:

0-40: High risk (50% kemungkinan default)
41-70: Medium risk (20% kemungkinan default)
71-100: Low risk (5% kemungkinan default)

Strategy:

Approve high credit score → Low risk, low return (bunga rendah)
Approve low credit score → High risk, high return (bunga tinggi)
Balance portfolio: Mix high & low risk

Key Metrics (Dashboard)
Balance: Rp 150,000,000           (Current cash)
Active Loans: 25                  (Loans sedang berjalan)
Total Disbursed: Rp 500,000,000   (All-time disbursed)
Total Repaid: Rp 350,000,000      (All-time repaid)
Default Rate: 4.5%                (NPL - Non Performing Loan)
Monthly Profit: Rp 15,000,000     (This month)
Win Condition

Keep Default Rate < 5%
Grow balance 10x (100 juta → 1 miliar)
Reach 1000 active customers
