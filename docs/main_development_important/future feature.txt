typescriptKoperasi Flow:

4. Collect loan repayments

├─ Members deposit Rp 1B total (3% interest)
├─ Loan out Rp 800M (10% interest)

├─ Expense: Rp 30M (deposit interest) + Rp 10M (operations)
├─ Surplus: Rp 40M
├─ Distribute: Rp 30M to members, Rp 10M reserve
└─ Member satisfaction increases = more deposits
