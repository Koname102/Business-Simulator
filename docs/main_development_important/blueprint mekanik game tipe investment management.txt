📊 BLUEPRINT: INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT (VC/PE/Mutual Fund)
Konsep Bisnis
Player adalah fund manager yang invest uang ke startup atau perusahaan lain. Dapat income dari management fee (% AUM) dan performance fee (% profit).
Cara Kerja (Real Life)
LP (Limited Partner) → Kasih uang ke fund (misal 50 miliar)
                     ↓
                Fund Manager (Player) → Invest ke startup
                     ↓
                 Startup → Grow atau fail
                     ↓
              Exit (IPO/M&A) → Dapat return (misal 3x)
                     ↓
             Return ke LP → Bagi hasil dengan LP
Gameplay Loop
1. DEAL FLOW (Investment Opportunities)
   - Setiap X detik, ada startup pitch
   - Tampil info: Nama, sector, valuation, equity stake offered
   
2. PLAYER DECISION (Investment)
   - INVEST → Uang keluar, dapat equity stake
   - PASS → Tidak ada perubahan
   
3. PORTFOLIO MONITORING
   - Setiap quarter, valuation berubah (naik/turun)
   - Random events affect startup (good/bad news)
   
4. EXIT OPPORTUNITIES
   - Startup IPO (dapat return besar)
   - M&A offer (dapat return medium)
   - Shutdown (total loss)
   
5. FEE COLLECTION
   - Management Fee: 2% AUM per tahun (passive income)
   - Performance Fee: 20% profit saat exit
Income Sources (Cara Dapat Uang)
SourceAmountTimingNotesManagement Fee2% AUM per tahunPer quarterStable incomePerformance Fee20% profitSaat exitBig windfallDividendVariesPer yearDari portfolio companies
Formula Income:
Management Fee (Annual) = AUM × 2%
Management Fee (Quarterly) = AUM × 0.5%

Contoh:
AUM: Rp 50 miliar
Management Fee (Annual) = 50,000,000,000 × 0.02 = Rp 1,000,000,000
Management Fee (Quarterly) = 1,000,000,000 / 4 = Rp 250,000,000

Performance Fee:
Investment: Rp 10 miliar (20% equity)
Exit Valuation: Rp 100 miliar
Exit Value: 100,000,000,000 × 0.20 = Rp 20 miliar
Profit: 20 miliar - 10 miliar = Rp 10 miliar
Performance Fee: 10,000,000,000 × 0.20 = Rp 2 miliar

Formula Expense:
Investment = Principal (saat invest)
Operational = Management Fee × 0.5
Failed Investment Loss = Principal - (Current Valuation × Equity Stake)
Risk Management
Portfolio Diversification:
- Invest di 10+ startups (don't put all eggs in one basket)
- Mix sectors: 30% Fintech, 30% E-commerce, 20% Healthtech, 20% Others
- Stage diversification: 50% early-stage, 30% growth, 20% late-stage
Success Rate:
Industry average:
- 50% total loss (fail)
- 30% break-even or small return (1-2x)
- 15% good return (3-5x)
- 5% home run (10x+)

Target portfolio IRR: 20-30% per year
Valuation Dynamics:
Early-stage: High volatility (-50% to +200% per quarter)
Growth-stage: Medium volatility (-20% to +50% per quarter)
Late-stage: Low volatility (-10% to +30% per quarter)
Key Metrics (Dashboard)
Balance: Rp 5,000,000,000

Active Investments: 12
Portfolio IRR: 25%
Realized Gains: Rp 15,000,000,000
Unrealized Gains: Rp 8,000,000,000

Win Condition

Grow AUM 10x (1 miliar → 10 miliar)
Achieve portfolio IRR > 25%
Exit 3 investments with 5x+ return
Raise Fund II (after Fund I success)
