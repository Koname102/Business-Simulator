📊 BLUEPRINT: INSURANCE (Asuransi)
Konsep Bisnis
Player adalah perusahaan asuransi yang jual polis. Customer bayar premi bulanan, dan ketika ada kejadian (sakit, kecelakaan, dll), mereka claim dan player bayar.
Cara Kerja (Real Life)
Customer → Beli Polis (coverage 100 juta, premi 500k/bulan)
         ↓
      Player → Terima premi bulanan
         ↓
  Incident → Customer kena musibah
         ↓
      Claim → Submit klaim (misal 50 juta)
         ↓
    Review → Approve atau reject
         ↓
   Approve → Bayar klaim (uang keluar)
Gameplay Loop
1. POLICY SALES
   - Setiap X detik, ada customer beli polis
   - Otomatis (player tidak perlu approve)
   - Premi masuk per bulan
   
2. PREMIUM COLLECTION
   - Setiap bulan (in-game), customer bayar premi
   - Ini adalah MAIN INCOME
   
3. CLAIM SUBMISSION (Random)
   - Ada kemungkinan customer submit claim
   - Tampil info: Nama, polis type, claim amount
   
4. PLAYER DECISION (Claim Review)
   - APPROVE → Bayar klaim (uang keluar)
   - REJECT → Customer unhappy (reputation turun)
   
5. POLICY EXPIRATION
   - Setelah 12 bulan, polis expire
   - Ada chance customer renew (jika reputation baik)
Income Sources (Cara Dapat Uang)

Formula Income:
Premium per Policy = Coverage × Rate × Risk Multiplier / 12

Contoh:
Coverage: Rp 100 juta
Base Rate: 2% per tahun
Risk Score: 50 (medium) → Multiplier 1.5x

Annual Premium = 100,000,000 × 0.02 × 1.5 = Rp 3,000,000
Monthly Premium = 3,000,000 / 12 = Rp 250,000

Investment Return:
Total Premium Collected: Rp 1 miliar
Invest di obligasi (8% per tahun)
Return: 1,000,000,000 × 0.08 = Rp 80,000,000/tahun

Formula Expense:
Claim Payment = Claim Amount (saat approve)
Operational = Total Premium × 15%
Reinsurance = Total Premium × 5%
Risk Management
Claim Ratio:
Claim Ratio = (Total Claims Paid / Total Premium Collected) × 100%

Target: < 70%
- Dibawah 50%: Too strict (reject too many, reputation drop)
- 50-70%: Ideal (profitable, customer happy)
- 70-85%: Danger zone (barely profitable)
- Diatas 85%: Bangkrut (losing money)
Claim Fraud Detection:

Setiap claim ada % chance fraud (5-20%)
Player harus decide: approve atau reject
Approve fraud = rugi, Reject valid = reputation turun

Keep Claim Ratio < 70%
Reach 10,000 active policies
Maintain Reputation > 80
Grow balance 10x
