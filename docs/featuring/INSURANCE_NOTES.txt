=== GAME LOOPS ===

Policy Sales (Every 20 seconds):
  - Auto-generate new policy
  - Random type: life/health/vehicle/property
  - Add to activePolicies
  - Show notification

Premium Collection (Every 15 seconds):
  - Loop through active policies
  - Collect monthly premium
  - Update balance (+income)
  - Update totalPremiumCollected
  - Check expiration (12 months)

Claim Generation (Every 25 seconds):
  - Random: Check each active policy
  - Probability:
    * Life: 1% (rare)
    * Health: 15% (frequent)
    * Vehicle: 10% (medium)
    * Property: 5% (rare but high amount)
  - Generate claim amount (% of coverage)
  - Add to pendingClaims

=== PLAYER DECISIONS ===

Approve Claim:
  - Balance decrease (expense)
  - Policy marked as 'claimed'
  - Customer happy
  - totalClaimsPaid increases

Reject Claim:
  - Balance unchanged
  - Reputation -5
  - Customer unhappy
  - Policy remains active

Strategy:
  - Approve valid claims: Customer satisfaction
  - Reject suspicious claims: Save money
  - Balance claim ratio <70%

=== KEY METRICS ===

Claim Ratio:
  - Formula: (totalClaims / totalPremium) × 100%
  - Target: <70%
  - 70-80%: Danger zone
  - >80%: Losing money

Active Policies:
  - Income source (monthly premium)
  - More policies = more premium
  - But also more claim risk

Premium vs Claims:
  - Premium: Recurring income (stable)
  - Claims: Random expense (volatile)
  - Profit = Premium - Claims - Operational

=== POLICY TYPES ===

Life Insurance:
  - Coverage: 50-500 million
  - Premium: 1.5% per year
  - Claim frequency: 1% (rare)
  - Claim amount: 80-100% (usually full)

Health Insurance:
  - Coverage: 10-100 million
  - Premium: 3% per year (highest)
  - Claim frequency: 15% (frequent)
  - Claim amount: 10-50% (medical bills)

Vehicle Insurance:
  - Coverage: 20-200 million
  - Premium: 2.5% per year
  - Claim frequency: 10% (medium)
  - Claim amount: 20-80% (repair costs)

Property Insurance:
  - Coverage: 100-1000 million (highest)
  - Premium: 2% per year
  - Claim frequency: 5% (rare)
  - Claim amount: 30-100% (disaster damage)

=== TIMINGS (Development) ===

Policy Sales: 20 seconds
Premium Collection: 15 seconds
Claim Generation: 25 seconds

Production (Recommended):
Policy Sales: 2 minutes
Premium Collection: 1 minute (= 1 month in-game)
Claim Generation: 3 minutes

=== BALANCE FLOW ===

Initial: Rp 500,000,000

Policy Sold (Premium 500k/month):
  Wait 15s → +500k (premium collected)
  
Claim Submitted (50 million):
  Approve → -50,000,000 (expense)
  Reject → Reputation -5

Example Flow (1 policy):
  Month 1: +500k premium
  Month 2: +500k premium
  Month 3: +500k premium, Claim 20M submitted
    → Approve: Balance = 500M + 1.5M - 20M = 481.5M
    → Profit = 1.5M - 20M = -18.5M (loss on this policy)

Example Flow (100 policies, avg 500k premium):
  Per month: +50M premium
  Claims (assume 10% claim, avg 10M each): -10M
  Net profit: +40M per month (healthy!)

=== WIN CONDITION ===

- Keep claim ratio <70%
- Maintain reputation >70
- Grow active policies to 1000+
- Reach balance 10x initial (5 billion)

=== ERROR HANDLING ===

Error: "Insufficient balance to pay claim"
  → Solution: Reject some claims or wait for premium

Error: "Reputation too low"
  → Solution: Approve more claims (customer satisfaction)

Error: "Claim ratio too high"
  → Solution: Reject more claims or increase premium (not implemented yet)
