- Real Life Insurance Logic:

1. Death Claim Approved:
- Pay claim: Rp 500M
- Policy status → 'claimed'
- Remove from active policies ✅
- Add to history: "Death claim approved - Paid Rp 500M"

Death Claim Rejected:
- No payment
- Policy status → 'terminated' ⚠️
- Remove from active policies ✅
- Add to history: "Death claim rejected - Not paid"
- Why? Person already dead, can't continue policy!
```

2. Critical Illness (Non-Terminal)
```
Person: ALIVE but sick 🏥
Payout: 30-50% coverage (Rp 150M - Rp 250M)
Policy After: STILL ACTIVE ✅
Premium: CONTINUE paying
Coverage: Reduced (Rp 500M → Rp 250M remaining)
```

3. Disability (Permanent)
```
Person: ALIVE but disabled ♿
Payout: 20-30% coverage (Rp 100M - Rp 150M)
Policy After: STILL ACTIVE with WAIVER ✅
Premium: WAIVED (don't need to pay anymore)
Coverage: CONTINUES (still protected)

- Active Policies Display:
ONLY:
✅ status = 'active' (paying premium, full coverage)
✅ status = 'premium_waiver' (not paying, still covered)

Hide (move to history):
❌ status = 'claimed' (death claim approved)
❌ status = 'expired' (contract ended)
❌ status = 'terminated' (death claim rejected)
