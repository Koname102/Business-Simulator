=== GAME LOOPS ===

Deal Generation (Every 30 seconds):
  - Generate startup pitch
  - Random sector (12 sectors)
  - Valuation: 10-100 billion
  - Equity offered: 10-30%
  - Investment needed: valuation × equity%
  - Add to deal flow

Valuation Updates (Every 20 seconds):
  - Loop through portfolio
  - Check failures (5-15% chance)
  - Check exit opportunities (5-30% chance)
  - Update valuations (±volatility)
  - Process exits if opportunity

Management Fee (Every 40 seconds):
  - Calculate 2% of AUM / 4 (quarterly)
  - Add to balance (income)
  - Recurring stable income

Market Condition (Every 60 seconds):
  - Random: Bull/Neutral/Bear
  - Bull: +5% bias on valuations
  - Bear: -5% bias on valuations
  - Show notification

=== PLAYER DECISIONS ===

Invest:
  - Review startup pitch
  - Check sector (high growth vs stable)
  - Check valuation (reasonable?)
  - Check equity % (enough ownership?)
  - Invest → Balance decreases
  - Add to portfolio

Pass:
  - Skip deal
  - No consequences
  - Wait for better opportunity

Manual Exit:
  - If return >2x: Can exit anytime
  - Get current valuation back
  - Performance fee: 20% of profit
  - Only when profitable

=== KEY METRICS ===

AUM (Asset Under Management):
  - Total portfolio value
  - Base for management fee
  - Example: 50B AUM → 250M fee/quarter

Portfolio IRR:
  - Overall return percentage
  - Target: >25% (good VC)
  - Industry benchmark: 20-30%

Return Multiple:
  - Current value / Initial investment
  - 1x = break even
  - 3x = good
  - 5x = excellent
  - 10x = unicorn

Realized vs Unrealized Gains:
  - Realized: Cash from exits
  - Unrealized: Paper profit (not sold)

=== INVESTMENT OUTCOMES ===

Success Distribution (Real VC):
  - 50% total loss (fail)
  - 30% break even or small return (1-2x)
  - 15% good return (3-5x)
  - 5% home run (10x+)

In-Game Probabilities:
  Failure:
    - Base: 5% per quarter
    - High risk sector: 8%
    - Low valuation: 15%
    - Minimum 6 months before can fail
  
  Exit Opportunity:
    - >5x return: 30% chance
    - >3x return: 20% chance
    - >2x return: 10% chance
    - <2x return: 5% chance
    - Minimum 12 months before can exit

=== VALUATION DYNAMICS ===

Volatility by Sector:
  High (30-35%):
    - Gaming, Social Media, SaaS
    - High growth potential
    - High failure risk
  
  Medium (15-25%):
    - E-commerce, Fintech, Healthtech, Edtech
    - Balanced risk/reward
  
  Low (10-15%):
    - Logistics, F&B, AgriTech
    - Stable, predictable
    - Lower returns

Market Conditions:
  Bull Market (+5% bias):
    - Valuations trend up
    - More exits
    - Good time to invest
  
  Bear Market (-5% bias):
    - Valuations trend down
    - Fewer exits
    - Wait for recovery

=== INCOME BREAKDOWN ===

Management Fee:
  - 2% of AUM per year
  - Paid quarterly (0.5% per quarter)
  - Stable recurring income
  - Example: 50B AUM → 250M/quarter
  - Total: 1B per year

Performance Fee:
  - 20% of profit on exits
  - One-time windfall
  - Main income source
  - Example: Exit 30B (invested 10B)
    * Profit: 20B
    * Performance fee: 4B
    * Net gain: 16B

Example Revenue (1 year):
  AUM: 50B
  Management fees: 4 quarters × 250M = 1B
  Exits: 2 investments
    - Investment A: 10B → 30B (profit 20B, fee 4B)
    - Investment B: 15B → 45B (profit 30B, fee 6B)
  Total revenue: 1B + 4B + 6B = 11B
  Total invested: 25B
  Net profit: 11B

=== STRATEGY TIPS ===

Diversification:
  - Invest in 5-10 startups minimum
  - Mix sectors (high risk + low risk)
  - Don't put all in one sector

Timing:
  - Invest in bull market (upward trend)
  - Hold during bear market (wait recovery)
  - Exit at >3x return (good profit)
  - Hold unicorns (10x+) longer

Risk Management:
  - Expect 50% to fail (normal VC)
  - 1 home run covers 10 failures
  - Focus on portfolio IRR, not individual

Initial: Rp 1,000,000,000,000 (1 trillion)

  Balance: 1T → 950B

Quarter 1:
  + Management fee: 500M
  + Valuations change: ±30%
  Balance: 950.5B

Quarter 4:
  + Management fee: 500M
  + Exit 1 startup: 30B (invested 10B)
    * Performance fee: 4B
  Balance: 950.5B + 500M + 30B + 4B = 985B

Year 2:
  + More exits: 3x 40B = 120B
  + Performance fees: 24B
  + Management fees: 2B
  Balance: 985B + 120B + 24B + 2B = 1.131T
  Profit: 131B (13.1% return)

=== WIN CONDITION ===

- Grow balance 5x (1T → 5T)
- Portfolio IRR >30%
- AUM >100B
- 5+ successful exits (3x+)

=== ERROR HANDLING ===

Error: "Insufficient balance to invest"
  → Wait for management fee
  → Wait for exit
  → Pass on expensive deals

Error: "Cannot exit at loss"
  → Wait for valuation recovery
  → Hold during bear market
  → Diversify to offset losses

Error: "Startup failed"
  → Normal! 50% fail
  → Diversify to reduce impact
  → Winners cover losers
